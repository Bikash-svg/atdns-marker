ℹ️ How this app works
A quick guide to setting up and using your attendance tracker.

1. Set your weekly schedule. Go to the Schedule tab and add the subjects you have on each day of the week. This tells the app what to expect on any given date.

2. Set your semester start date. Below, under Semester start, pick the date your classes began. Attendance is only counted from that date onward.

3. Mark attendance daily. On the Today tab, mark each scheduled class as attended, missed, or dismissed. You can also tap any past date on the calendar to mark or edit that day, and add an extra (unscheduled) class if one happened.

4. Check your stats. The Today tab shows your overall attendance ring, and the Subjects tab breaks it down subject-by-subject so you know where you stand.

5. Customize it. Right here in Settings you can switch light/dark mode, pick a color scheme, and back up or restore your data.

Everything is stored locally on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

📦 Backup & Restore
Export your data as a JSON file, or import a previously saved backup.
Backup saved. Want to send it somewhere?

Export saves all your data — schedule, attendance records, extra classes, and semester start date — as a .json file to your phone's Documents folder, visible in your file manager app. If your device supports it, a Share option will also appear so you can send the file directly via WhatsApp, email, Drive, or any other app.

Import replaces all current data with the contents of a previously exported file. Warning: this overwrites everything, so export first if you need to keep the current data.

Your data stays on your device — no internet or cloud storage is used.
